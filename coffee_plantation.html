Coffee Production Process

The five main stages of coffee production are:

  1. Planting
  2. Cultivation and maintenance
  3. Harvesting
  4. Processing and drying
  5. Packaging

Coffee Plantation Activities

Pruning
Removing selected branches from coffee plants to encourage healthy growth and productivity.
Harvesting
Picking ripe coffee cherries from coffee plants when they are ready for processing.
Drying
Reducing moisture in processed coffee beans to preserve their quality before storage and packaging.
